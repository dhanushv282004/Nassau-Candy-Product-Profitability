4. Product-level monthly margins show no observed volatility in the supplied 2025 data.
5. Cost and sales are strongly correlated at aggregated product level.

### Implications
Use the Streamlit dashboard to monitor major profit contributors and investigate margin-risk candidates. Pair margin percentage with absolute profit contribution when assessing product performance.

This summary is descriptive and should not be treated as causal evidence.
